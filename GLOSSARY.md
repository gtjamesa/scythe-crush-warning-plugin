# Scythe Crush Warning

Warns when a weapon that can attack on crush is on the wrong attack style for where the player is.

## Language

**Region**:
A 64x64-tile square of the game map, identified by its region ID.

**Crush region**:
A region where the player expects to attack on crush. It is either a boss region or a custom region.
_Avoid_: Allowed region, ignored region

**Boss region**:
A crush region that ships with the plugin and is turned on per boss.

**Custom region**:
A crush region the player marks themselves in game.
_Avoid_: Ignored region, user region
